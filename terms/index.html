A clear starting point.

The Inetixus workspace is a place to discuss Roblox development work, request quotes, and review progress.

Requests and quotes

Sending a request does not place an order or take a payment. Scope, pricing, payment arrangements, deadlines, and delivery are agreed directly before work starts. If a request changes, use the conversation or quote section to agree on the additional work.

Project conversations

Use the workspace for your own projects and information you have permission to share. Keep account credentials private and do not send passwords through the chat.

Reviews and changes

GUI revisions and adjustments are included for 14 days after delivery as described in the portfolio. New screens, features, or systems can require a separate quote.
