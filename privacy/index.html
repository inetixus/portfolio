Your work. Your privacy.

This page explains the information used by the Inetixus portfolio and project workspace.

Account and project information

The workspace uses your email address, display name, project briefs, messages, quote requests, and progress updates to manage your projects. Google sign-in provides the profile information needed to create or access your account. Your Google password is not shared with Inetixus.

Who can access it

Project information is available to the signed-in client for that project and the Inetixus owner account. Supabase provides authentication and database services. Vercel hosts the public website. An email delivery provider sends account verification and recovery messages.

Your browser

The workspace stores its login session in your browser so you can stay signed in. It also temporarily stores an unfinished message draft, your selected package, and the email address awaiting verification. Log out on shared devices.

Contact and data requests

For questions about your information or requests to access, correct, or delete account and project data, email inetixus@gmail.com. Avoid sending passwords or other credentials through project messages.
